ULTIMATE OBSERVATORY TELEMETRY GLOSSARY (15 DIALS)

1. Boyle Index (CPCP)

Estimates Polar Cap Potential drop in kilovolts (kV). Green <150 kV, Yellow 150–200 kV, Red >=200 kV.

2. IMF Clock Angle (theta)

Orientation of IMF in GSM Y-Z plane. 0 deg North, 180 deg South.

3. Solar Wind Velocity (v)

Bulk speed of proton plasma from Sun past L1 in km/s.

4. Proton Density (n)

Number of solar wind protons per cm³.

5. Ram Pressure (P_ram)

Kinetic pressure exerted by solar wind stream in nPa.

6. Alfvén Mach Number (M_A)

Ratio of solar wind speed to Alfvén wave speed.

7. Gas Mach Number (M_g)

Ratio of solar wind bulk speed to thermal sound speed.

8. IMF Bz Component

North-South magnetic field component in nT.

9. Vx Earthward Velocity

Direct Earthward component (Vx) of solar wind velocity vector in km/s.

10. Proton Temperature (Tp)

Thermal temperature of solar wind protons in Kelvin (K).

11. Total Magnetic Field (Bt)

Magnitude of total interplanetary magnetic field vector in nT.

12. GIC & Telluric Risk (dE/dt)

Composite rate-of-energy transfer index (0-100).

13. Planetary K-Index (Kp)

Global geomagnetic activity index (0-9).

14. Dawn-Dusk Electric Field (Ey)

Interplanetary electric field (mV/m).

15. Magnetopause Standoff (Rmp)

Dayside boundary distance of Earth's magnetic shield in Earth Radii (RE).
